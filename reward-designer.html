The agent does exactly what you pay it for

Round 1, Formulate it (40 points): four situations. Pick the right state, action, reward or episode type. 15 seconds each.

Round 2, Fix the reward (60 points): the Campus Courier robot must carry books from the hostel to the library without crossing the rain puddle. Three broken reward designs. Change the sliders, press Train, see where the robot goes, then Submit.

Remember the cobra bounty: pay for dead cobras and people breed cobras. An agent does the same with a careless reward.

Training here is exact (value iteration), so the same sliders always give the same robot. How an agent learns this by trial and error is Q-learning, in the next lecture.

What to take from it

Pay for results, not activity. +1 per step made staying out worth 1/(1 − γ) = 20, more than the library.

Write down every cost you care about. If wet books are bad, the reward has to say so, and say it more loudly than the extra steps cost. With step −1 and γ = 0.95, the puddle reward must be below about −6.

γ < 1 makes sooner better. With no step cost and γ = 1, every route to the library is worth the same, so the robot need not arrive.
