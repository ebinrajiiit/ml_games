\begin{itemize}
  \item The plain RNN does better than its reputation up to $T=40$, then collapses to guessing.
  \item The gated cells hold the key for 80 steps, and the GRU for 160.
  \item One LSTM seed failed at $T=160$: training long memories is still not guaranteed.
\end{itemize}
